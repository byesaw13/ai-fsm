Dana sees only the address and the work she paid for, never the homeowner's other records.

Why it's laid out this way
  • Most customers will only ever see screen 1, because it arrives by text with no login. Two out of three of your customers have no email on file, so today they can't log in at all. The redesign adds sign-in by text.
  • "Keep for your records" lists only what you tick at closeout (paint, fixtures), never joint compound or dump fees.
  • The home record is like Bodie, but it fills itself: each job you publish adds an entry with an area and "improvement / repair / maintenance", and the PDF is what a seller hands a buyer or insurer.
  • Several addresses: switch between them at the top. Invoices are grouped by address, each with its own total and "Select all" for printing.
  • Realtors: "Work you paid for" is grouped by client address, with a per-address total, job reports and a home-record PDF. "Request work" lets them say "bill me".
  • Portal order is: what you owe → ask for work → newest job → what's coming → history → records → invoices.
