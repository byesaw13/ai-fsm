## Problem Statement
The customer "vault" feels empty because it is empty. Production has 0 rows in property_vault_items,
property_issues, property_notes, property_condition_snapshots, and maintenance_plans. It depends on
manual data entry that never happens. Meanwhile the evidence customers actually want already exists
and is never shown to them:

- 217 job photos tied to properties (150 after, 28 before, 44 assessment).
- 35 visits with tech notes.
- 175 customer-visible invoice lines naming real products (e.g. "Benjamin Moore Regal Select White Dove eggshell").
- 13 invoices with work_summary.
- 18 work orders.

The portal shows no photos, and customers have logged in 3 times ever.
The portal also gives customers no way to do anything themselves:
- They can't ask for work.
- They can't fix their own phone or email.
- They can't see what they've spent.
- They can't print more than one invoice at a time.

## What Makes This Cool
The customer gets a record of their house that builds itself from the work. Each finished job becomes a short
"Job Report" they can open from a text message without logging in: before and after photos, what was done in
each room, and exactly what was installed (brand and color). Over time the portal becomes the house's
service history. It's the thing they forward to a realtor or buyer, and the reason they call Dovetails first.

## Constraints
- Owner is the only publisher; nothing reaches a customer without a tap (trust + photo quality control).
- No new data entry: everything is pre-filled from photos, visit notes, invoice lines, work_summary.
- Must follow portal rules already shipped:
  - Preview sessions are read-only (TASK-160).
  - Sponsored work is scoped per realtor (TASK-158/159).
  - Redirects go through `appUrl()`.
- Memberships / maintenance plans stay deferred; PRODUCT_VISION unchanged — the portal remains a feature, not a product line.
- Roadmap table freeze: this needs ONE new table (`portal_job_updates`). An explicit Phase 2 exception must be
  written into ROADMAP.md before build, the same way TASK-115/158 were.

## Premises (agreed)
1. The vault builds itself from completed work. It does not depend on manual vault entry.
2. The customer page answers three questions, in this order:
   - **What did you do?** Published Job Reports, newest first, with before/after photos and room-by-room notes.
   - **What's in my house?** Installed items rolled up from published billed product lines.
   - **What's next?** Open estimates, recommendations from reports, the next scheduled visit, and a "Request service" button.
   - Invoices come after these, lower on the page.
3. The owner publishes each job at closeout. The report is pre-filled and takes one tap; there is a reminder for finished jobs that haven't been published.
4. Memberships later. Vision doc unchanged.

- **A: Fill the existing vault tables** — rejected. They are 0 rows because they need data entry; filling them again repeats the failure.

### Our production reality (read-only queries)
- **65% of live clients (49/75) have no email — and portal login is email-only.** Two-thirds of customers can never sign in. Only 3 real portal sessions, ever.
- 23 of 36 visit tech notes are filler ("Auto-created from confirmed on-site stop", "Same as above"). Unusable as customer text.
- Invoice lines are mostly consumables ("USG All Purpose Joint Compound", "SP Wasp & Hornet", "Travel (T&M)", dump fees). Auto-rolling them into "In your home" would be junk.
- Photos: 150 after, 44 assessment, 28 before. Before/after *pairs* are rare.
- Only 13 of 32 sent invoices have a work_summary.
- Current portal (screenshot, demo client): estimates render as "9/24/2026 · $200.00 · Approved · View →" repeated — no clue what the work was.

## Readjustments from round 2
1. **Text sign-in (new, highest priority, in TASK-161).** "Text me a link" on the portal login using the phone on file and the existing SMS gateway (`sendSmsViaGateway`), same magic-link rules as email. Without it the portal is invisible to 65% of customers.
2. **Summary pre-fill ignores tech notes.** Order: invoice work_summary → the job title + customer-visible *labor/service* line names as bullets → blank box. The owner writes 1–2 sentences; the page shows a good example.
3. **"In your home" becomes "Keep for your records" and is opt-in per line.** At publish, lines are listed with nothing ticked; the owner ticks lasting items (paint, fixtures, materials installed) and can add a free-text item ("Hall + stairs: BM White Dove, eggshell"). Consumables, travel, and dumping never default in.
4. **Photos lead with "after".** Before/after side-by-side only when both exist; otherwise an after gallery. Field nudge: capture one "before" on arrival (separate, later).
5. **Portal top = status, not archive.** Order: balance-due banner (if any) → Request service button → latest Job Report → What's next (estimates *with titles*, next visit) → All reports → Keep for your records → Invoices (select & print, lifetime spend) → Your info.
6. **Estimate rows show what the work is** (job title or first scope line + address). Tiny fix, rides with TASK-164.
7. **Home record, Bodie-style (round 3, owner approved).** The property page is a home record of Dovetails work only:
   a timeline of published reports plus spending by area. Publishing adds two quick picks:
   - **area:** Interior paint, Exterior, Deck/porch, Carpentry, Plumbing, Electrical, Roof/gutters, Yard, Other;
   - **type:** improvement, repair, or maintenance.

   Improvements can add to the home's cost basis at sale, which gives the homeowner a real reason to keep the record.
8. **Home Record report (TASK-165).** One PDF or share link per property. It shows:
   - the address;
   - totals by area and by type;
   - each job: date, title, one after photo, and its records items.

   Uses: sale, insurance, refinance. A realtor who paid for listing prep can hand it to buyers. Either the owner or the
   homeowner can generate it. It never includes invoices billed to someone else.
9. **Not copied from Bodie:**
   - homeowner uploads of other contractors' work;
   - a document library for deeds and insurance;
   - a vendor directory;
   - subscriptions.

   Together those make a separate homeowner SaaS, which the canonical docs exclude, and they depend on customers
   entering data. The natural v2 is warranty dates on records items with expiry nudges. That leads into maintenance
   reminders, which stay deferred with memberships.

## Recommended Approach: C — Job Report link

### 1. Publish at closeout (owner side)
- `portal_job_updates` table:
  - id, account_id, job_id (unique), property_id, client_id, share_token (uuid).
  - summary text, area text, work_type text (improvement | repair | maintenance), media_ids uuid[], installed_line_ids uuid[].
  - status: draft | published | withdrawn.
  - published_at, first_viewed_at, view_count.
  - RLS policy and coverage guard, like every account table.
- `/app/jobs/[jobId]/customer-update` review page. It is pre-filled with:
  - summary ← invoice work_summary, else job title + labor/service line names as bullets, else blank (never tech notes — 64% are filler);
  - photos ← `visit_media` rows for the job's visits, categories before/after preselected (assessment off by default; `receipt` never offered and rejected by the media route);
  - "Keep for your records" ← customer-visible lines listed **unticked**, plus a free-text item box.
  The owner unticks anything, edits the summary, and taps **Publish & text** (or email).
- A "Finished, not published" count appears on My Day / Needs Attention; the owner can also mark a job **Skip**.
- **Past-jobs queue:** the same page, listed for completed jobs with photos and no update. The owner works through Kim and Norman first.

### 2. Job Report page (customer side, no login)
- Lives at `/portal/reports/[token]`, modelled on invoice share tokens.
- Shows before/after photos (tap to enlarge), summary, installed items, the date, and the property address.
- Has a link back to the full portal.
- Tracks views the same way invoices do (not counted for staff or preview sessions).
- **Recipient rule:** publishing requires the job to have an invoice with `status NOT IN ('draft','void')`. `portal_job_updates.client_id` = that
  invoice's `client_id` (the payer). If the job's invoices disagree (mixed payers across progress bills), publish is
  blocked with a message — owner picks. When the payer invoice has `billing_context='realtor_sponsored'`, the
  report shows only the minimum property identity; its record items do NOT feed the realtor's "Keep for your records"
  rollup (that section only covers properties the client owns: `properties.client_id` = client OR an `owner`-role `property_contacts` row — never realtor/beneficiary rows). Sponsored reports appear only inside the existing Sponsored Work section, not under "What we did". The beneficiary
  homeowner does not receive sponsored reports in v1.
- **Token hygiene:** `noindex`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; withdraw rotates the token and the old one 404s.
  Photos are served only via `/portal/reports/[token]/media/[mediaId]`, which re-checks the id is in the published
  row's `media_ids` — never raw media URLs.
- **Array integrity:** `media_ids` / `installed_line_ids` have no FKs; the publish endpoint validates every id
  belongs to this job and account, and the renderer silently skips ids whose rows were later deleted.
- No Request service on the no-login report page in v1 (a forwarded link would let anyone file against the property);
  it links to the portal instead.

### 3. Portal redesign (`/portal/[clientToken]`)
Sections in order:
1. Balance-due banner (only if something is owed) + **Request service** button.
2. Latest Job Report card.
3. What's next (open estimates showing job title/address, next visit).
4. All reports (newest first).
5. Keep for your records (ticked items + free-text, per owned property).
6. Invoices — select & print, lifetime spend.
7. Your info.

### 4. Self-service (the user's asks: "request services, edit their information, … see their life time spending, … select 1 or multiple invoices and print them")

| Feature | Build on | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Request service** | Existing `app/api/booking/route.ts` booking-request pipeline (already bridges to prefilled estimate) | Portal form pre-filled with the client's name and contact. It adds a property picker (their properties plus "a different address") and a description. It creates a booking_request with the existing `client_id`/`property_id` columns (mig 021) set, so staff don't retype anything. Rate-limited. |
| **Edit my info** | New `PATCH /api/portal/[clientToken]/profile` | Requires `getPortalSession()` whose clientId matches the token (a portal_token alone never authorizes a write); 403 in preview. Edits phone and preferred contact directly. **Email change** is a login-identity change: send a verify link to the new address (additive `pending_email` column on `portal_magic_links`, mig 067 — no new table), apply only on click, notify the old address. Name/address: v1 shows "call or text us to change this" (no new request type). Every change notifies the owner and is audit-logged. |
| **Lifetime spend** | SUM(paid_cents + GREATEST(COALESCE(deposit_cents,0),0)) over the client's non-draft, non-void invoices (same paid math as mig 193), including invoices they paid as a sponsor | Shows total paid and year-to-date. Uses paid amounts, not billed amounts, so it matches bank records. |
| **Select & print invoices** | `lib/pdf/load.ts` loaders + pdf-lib | Checkboxes on the invoice list with Select all and Print selected. `GET /api/portal/[clientToken]/invoices.pdf?ids=…` merges each invoice's existing PDF into one file. Predicate: `id = ANY($ids) AND client_id = $sessionClient AND account_id = $acct AND status <> 'draft'`; reject unless matched count = ids.length (sponsor-paid invoices already carry the sponsor as client_id). Cap 10 per request to bound render time. |

### 5. Several addresses and realtor clients (round 4, owner request)
- **Clients with more than one address:** a row of address chips at the top of the portal ("All addresses" plus one per
  property). The home record, Request service, and What's next follow the selected address.
- **Invoices are grouped by address:**
  - taken from `invoices.property_id`, falling back to the job's property;
  - 4 sent invoices in production have no address, and they go in a "No address yet" group;
  - each group has its own paid total, amount due, and "Select all" for the combined PDF.
- **Realtor portal (TASK-166)**, for any client who has `realtor_sponsored` invoices billed to them:
  - "Work you paid for", grouped by client address, with paid and due amounts per address, links to the job reports,
    and a home-record PDF limited to the work *they* paid for at that address;
  - "Request work for a client's home": address, what's needed, access notes (lockbox), **Who pays? Bill me / Bill the
    homeowner**, homeowner name (optional).
    - It uses the existing `booking_requests.address`, `access_notes` and `service_description` columns, plus one
      additive column `bill_to_requester boolean`.
    - When staff turn a "bill me" request into an invoice, it defaults to `realtor_sponsored`.
  - Scoping is unchanged from TASK-158/159: the realtor sees only the address and the work they paid for, never the
    homeowner's other records.
  - If the realtor also owns a home with us, it appears as a normal address chip.

## Success Criteria
- At least 80% of jobs completed after launch are published within 48 h.
- Kim's and Norman's past jobs are published through the queue.
- At least 50% of texted Job Reports are opened (view_count), against a baseline of 3 portal logins ever.
- At least 1 service request comes through the portal within 30 days.
- Zero customer-visible leaks:
  - no unpublished photos;
  - no cross-client invoice in a combined PDF;
  - preview stays read-only;
  - a sponsored report leaks no installed items or vault data to the realtor;
  - profile PATCH rejects token-without-session;
  - a withdrawn/rotated report token 404s;
  - a media id outside the published set 404s.
  Each of these is covered by an integration test.
